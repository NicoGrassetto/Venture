# Design a Business Model: Method Reference

Use this reference when the core workflow requires deeper analysis. Keep the final artifact concise and move raw evidence to linked notes or data files.

## Decision objective

A selected business model with explicit value flows, payer, revenue unit, cost drivers, incentives, risks, and experiment plan.

## Method notes

- Examples of charge bases include subscription, transaction, usage, licensing, service, marketplace take rate, product plus consumables, and outcome share.
- Evaluate models as systems: payer, user, timing, risk, ownership, and channel must fit together.
- Use rough unit economics now; exact pricing comes later, but models that cannot plausibly work should be rejected.

## Review questions

- Can the team demonstrate: the model names a payer and a chargeable unit.
- Can the team demonstrate: free usage has an explicit funding mechanism.
- Can the team demonstrate: channel and partner incentives are economically coherent.
- Can the team demonstrate: the selected model can plausibly support attractive LTV and COCA.

Also ask:

- What observation would make the current conclusion wrong?
- Which input has the greatest leverage on the decision?
- Is the evidence from the actual beachhead and actual decision makers?
- Have incentives, selection bias, and survivorship bias been considered?
- Is the next action the cheapest action that can materially reduce uncertainty?
