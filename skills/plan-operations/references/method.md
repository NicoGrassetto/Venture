# Plan Operations: Method Reference

## Capacity before promises

Define one delivery unit and period. Required hours equal demand units times
hours per unit. Gap hours equal the positive part of required minus available
hours. Labor cost equals required hours times fully loaded hourly cost.

These are planning approximations, not queueing guarantees. Account explicitly
for setup, rework, variability, training, and quality checks. Validate estimates
against observed delivery before increasing commitments.

## Cost and ownership

Separate fulfillment costs, acquisition costs, ongoing overhead, and capital
expenditure. Give each cost one home in the financial model. A person's unpaid
time still limits capacity and has an opportunity cost.

Name a responsible role for each delivery activity and dependency. Record
supplier lead time, the signal that failure is developing, and an actionable
fallback. Do not assume a second vendor is available without evidence.

## Review

Test a demand spike, a delayed supplier, and an absent operator. If no feasible
response exists, change the scope or promise instead of writing an optimistic
contingency paragraph. Review legal and regulated obligations with qualified
professionals.
