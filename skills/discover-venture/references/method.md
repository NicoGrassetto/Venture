# Discover a Venture: Method Reference

Use this reference when the core workflow requires deeper analysis. Keep the final artifact concise and move raw evidence to linked notes or data files.

## Decision objective

A venture thesis with a clearly stated source, founder motivation, initial capabilities, team gaps, and a short list of ideas ready for market segmentation.

## Method notes

- Use a founder-origin matrix with rows for idea, technology, and passion/capability. Record what is known, what is assumed, and what evidence each origin still requires.
- Use a weighted score only to structure debate; it is not proof. Preserve dissenting notes and sensitivity-test any heavily weighted criterion.
- For team design, distinguish missing skills that can be hired from missing founder-level ownership, credibility, or long-term commitment.

## Review questions

- Can the team demonstrate: the thesis names a customer context and problem, not only a product.
- Can the team demonstrate: at least one founder has credible access to prospective users or domain evidence.
- Can the founder demonstrate: cofounders have discussed commitment, roles, and conflict expectations, or a solo founder has documented capacity and ownership gaps.
- Can the team demonstrate: the team can explain why it will spend the next several years on this problem.

Also ask:

- What observation would make the current conclusion wrong?
- Which input has the greatest leverage on the decision?
- Is the evidence from the actual beachhead and actual decision makers?
- Have incentives, selection bias, and survivorship bias been considered?
- Is the next action the cheapest action that can materially reduce uncertainty?
