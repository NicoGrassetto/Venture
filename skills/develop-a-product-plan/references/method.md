# Develop a Product Plan: Method Reference

Use this reference when the core workflow requires deeper analysis. Keep the final artifact concise and move raw evidence to linked notes or data files.

## Decision objective

A product and market evolution plan with horizons, dependencies, learning gates, resource implications, and explicit protection of beachhead execution.

## Method notes

- Use now/next/later or dated horizons only when timing confidence supports dates.
- Define a gate as measurable evidence required before committing the next tranche of resources.
- Prioritize enabling capabilities when they unlock several validated outcomes, but avoid speculative platform work.

## Review questions

- Can the team demonstrate: near-term work improves beachhead value, reliability, retention, or economics.
- Can the team demonstrate: follow-on initiatives have explicit market and evidence triggers.
- Can the team demonstrate: features trace to customer outcomes or enabling capabilities.
- Can the team demonstrate: the plan respects real resource and dependency constraints.

Also ask:

- What observation would make the current conclusion wrong?
- Which input has the greatest leverage on the decision?
- Is the evidence from the actual beachhead and actual decision makers?
- Have incentives, selection bias, and survivorship bias been considered?
- Is the next action the cheapest action that can materially reduce uncertainty?
