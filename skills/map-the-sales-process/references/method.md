# Map the Sales Process: Method Reference

Use this reference when the core workflow requires deeper analysis. Keep the final artifact concise and move raw evidence to linked notes or data files.

## Decision objective

A staged seller-side process with channels, activities, conversion assumptions, costs, ownership, evidence, and evolution from early founder sales to scale.

## Method notes

- For each stage, define a customer-observable milestone rather than an internal activity count.
- Model multiple acquisition paths separately when channels have materially different costs or conversion.
- Use the map to generate measurable funnel assumptions for top-down COCA.

## Review questions

- Can the team demonstrate: seller stages align with how customers actually buy.
- Can the team demonstrate: each stage has measurable entry and exit criteria.
- Can the team demonstrate: founder-led exceptions are not treated as the scalable steady state.
- Can the team demonstrate: costs and conversion assumptions can feed the COCA model.

Also ask:

- What observation would make the current conclusion wrong?
- Which input has the greatest leverage on the decision?
- Is the evidence from the actual beachhead and actual decision makers?
- Have incentives, selection bias, and survivorship bias been considered?
- Is the next action the cheapest action that can materially reduce uncertainty?
