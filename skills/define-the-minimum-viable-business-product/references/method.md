# Define the Minimum Viable Business Product: Method Reference

Use this reference when the core workflow requires deeper analysis. Keep the final artifact concise and move raw evidence to linked notes or data files.

## Decision objective

A tightly scoped MVBP specification with customer, value, payment, instrumentation, service boundaries, and learning goals.

## Method notes

- Use a scope trace: every included element must enable customer value, payment, feedback, safety, or a critical assumption test.
- Treat concierge and manual operations as deliberate learning mechanisms with owners and capacity limits.
- Define what will be learned from both successful and unsuccessful usage before launch.

## Review questions

- Can the team demonstrate: a customer can obtain meaningful value from the complete workflow.
- Can the team demonstrate: payment or an equivalent binding commercial commitment is built into the test.
- Can the team demonstrate: usage and outcome feedback can be measured.
- Can the team demonstrate: manual work is explicit, safe, and not mistaken for scalable economics.

Also ask:

- What observation would make the current conclusion wrong?
- Which input has the greatest leverage on the decision?
- Is the evidence from the actual beachhead and actual decision makers?
- Have incentives, selection bias, and survivorship bias been considered?
- Is the next action the cheapest action that can materially reduce uncertainty?
