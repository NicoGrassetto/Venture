# Profile the Persona: Method Reference

Use this reference when the core workflow requires deeper analysis. Keep the final artifact concise and move raw evidence to linked notes or data files.

## Decision objective

A fact-based primary persona tied to a real person, with observable goals, context, behavior, and prioritized purchasing criteria.

## Method notes

- Ask for recent specific examples, then inspect artifacts or workflows when possible.
- Use forced ranking or pairwise comparison to reveal purchasing priorities; a flat list is not actionable.
- Keep the persona stable enough to align the team, but revise it when systematic evidence - not one opinion - shows a mismatch.

## Review questions

- Can the team demonstrate: the persona is based on a real target user, not a fictional composite alone.
- Can the team demonstrate: purchasing criteria are explicitly ranked.
- Can the team demonstrate: the profile includes emotional and social motivations as well as functional needs.
- Can the team demonstrate: the persona changes at least one real product, messaging, or research decision.

Also ask:

- What observation would make the current conclusion wrong?
- Which input has the greatest leverage on the decision?
- Is the evidence from the actual beachhead and actual decision makers?
- Have incentives, selection bias, and survivorship bias been considered?
- Is the next action the cheapest action that can materially reduce uncertainty?
