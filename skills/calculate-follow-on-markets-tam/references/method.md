# Calculate Follow-on Markets TAM: Method Reference

Use this reference when the core workflow requires deeper analysis. Keep the final artifact concise and move raw evidence to linked notes or data files.

## Decision objective

A directional broader-TAM model and prioritized adjacency roadmap linked to transferable product, channel, brand, data, and capability advantages.

## Method notes

- Use the same unit discipline as beachhead TAM, but accept wider ranges because follow-on evidence is earlier.
- Score transferability and incremental burden separately; a large market with little leverage may be a poor next move.
- Sequence markets so each entry creates assets or learning that make the next entry easier.

## Review questions

- Can the team demonstrate: follow-on markets are separate from beachhead TAM.
- Can the team demonstrate: each adjacency has a clear transfer mechanism from the beachhead.
- Can the team demonstrate: sizing uses visible assumptions and appropriately broad ranges.
- Can the team demonstrate: the roadmap preserves near-term beachhead focus.

Also ask:

- What observation would make the current conclusion wrong?
- Which input has the greatest leverage on the decision?
- Is the evidence from the actual beachhead and actual decision makers?
- Have incentives, selection bias, and survivorship bias been considered?
- Is the next action the cheapest action that can materially reduce uncertainty?
