# Validate Customer Traction: Method Reference

Use this reference when the core workflow requires deeper analysis. Keep the final artifact concise and move raw evidence to linked notes or data files.

## Decision objective

An evidence-based product-consumption assessment with cohort trends, payment proof, customer outcomes, root causes, and a scale/iterate/pivot decision.

## Method notes

- Define activation as the earliest behavior strongly associated with receiving the core value.
- Use cohort retention curves and segment by acquisition source or customer type when sample size permits.
- Triangulate telemetry, payment records, customer outcomes, and interviews; no single source is sufficient.

## Review questions

- Can the team demonstrate: metrics reflect meaningful customer value.
- Can the team demonstrate: real customers use and pay, or a documented binding payment path exists.
- Can the team demonstrate: retention and repeated use are analyzed by cohort.
- Can the team demonstrate: the team acts on negative evidence with intellectual honesty.

Also ask:

- What observation would make the current conclusion wrong?
- Which input has the greatest leverage on the decision?
- Is the evidence from the actual beachhead and actual decision makers?
- Have incentives, selection bias, and survivorship bias been considered?
- Is the next action the cheapest action that can materially reduce uncertainty?
