# Calculate Beachhead Market TAM: Method Reference

Use this reference when the core workflow requires deeper analysis. Keep the final artifact concise and move raw evidence to linked notes or data files.

## Decision objective

A transparent annual revenue TAM range for the beachhead, with reproducible assumptions, sources, sensitivity analysis, and reconciliation.

## Method notes

- Core formula: annual beachhead TAM = addressable economic units x annual revenue per unit.
- If several customer types or price tiers exist, calculate each cohort separately and sum them.
- Sensitivity-test the two or three assumptions with the largest effect; do not vary every input mechanically.

## Review questions

- Can the team demonstrate: tAM represents annual revenue at 100% beachhead share, not company valuation or global industry spend.
- Can the team demonstrate: the bottom-up model can be recalculated from visible inputs.
- Can the team demonstrate: counts and price use compatible units and time periods.
- Can the team demonstrate: top-down filters end at the same market definition as the bottom-up model.

Also ask:

- What observation would make the current conclusion wrong?
- Which input has the greatest leverage on the decision?
- Is the evidence from the actual beachhead and actual decision makers?
- Have incentives, selection bias, and survivorship bias been considered?
- Is the next action the cheapest action that can materially reduce uncertainty?
