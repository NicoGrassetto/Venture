# Define the Product Concept: Method Reference

Use this reference when the core workflow requires deeper analysis. Keep the final artifact concise and move raw evidence to linked notes or data files.

## Decision objective

A shared, testable product concept expressed through visuals, key workflows, boundaries, and benefit-led messaging.

## Method notes

- Use the lowest fidelity that lets customers react to the value and workflow.
- During concept tests, ask participants to explain what they think it does before answering questions.
- Separate comprehension, desirability, usability, feasibility, and willingness-to-pay findings; one does not prove the others.

## Review questions

- Can the team demonstrate: a target customer can explain the product and benefit after reviewing the artifact.
- Can the team demonstrate: features trace to persona priorities or lifecycle barriers.
- Can the team demonstrate: the artifact is cheap to revise and does not imply false implementation certainty.
- Can the team demonstrate: non-goals prevent feedback from expanding scope silently.

Also ask:

- What observation would make the current conclusion wrong?
- Which input has the greatest leverage on the decision?
- Is the evidence from the actual beachhead and actual decision makers?
- Have incentives, selection bias, and survivorship bias been considered?
- Is the next action the cheapest action that can materially reduce uncertainty?
