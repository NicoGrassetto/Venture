# Set Your Pricing Framework: Method Reference

Use this reference when the core workflow requires deeper analysis. Keep the final artifact concise and move raw evidence to linked notes or data files.

## Decision objective

A testable pricing architecture tied to customer value, segment differences, purchase friction, and the chosen business model.

## Method notes

- Estimate value captured as price divided by credible realized customer value, not headline theoretical value.
- Use good-better-best packaging only when differences map to real customer needs and economics.
- Prioritize revealed preference - signed proposal, deposit, paid pilot - over survey statements.

## Review questions

- Can the team demonstrate: the metric correlates with realized customer value.
- Can the team demonstrate: a customer can understand and forecast the charge.
- Can the team demonstrate: discounts exchange for explicit value and do not silently reset reference price.
- Can the team demonstrate: the framework is consistent with the business model and buying process.

Also ask:

- What observation would make the current conclusion wrong?
- Which input has the greatest leverage on the decision?
- Is the evidence from the actual beachhead and actual decision makers?
- Have incentives, selection bias, and survivorship bias been considered?
- Is the next action the cheapest action that can materially reduce uncertainty?
