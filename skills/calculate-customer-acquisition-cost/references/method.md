# Calculate Customer Acquisition Cost: Method Reference

Use this reference when the core workflow requires deeper analysis. Keep the final artifact concise and move raw evidence to linked notes or data files.

## Decision objective

A period- and cohort-aligned COCA model including all acquisition costs, with channel views, payback analysis, and reduction priorities.

## Method notes

- Core formula: COCA = fully loaded sales and marketing acquisition spend / newly acquired paying customers.
- For long sales cycles, use lagged cohorts or pipeline-weighted attribution rather than same-month division.
- Calculate cash payback from cumulative contribution after acquisition; a ratio alone misses financing needs.

## Review questions

- Can the team demonstrate: the numerator includes all material acquisition costs.
- Can the team demonstrate: the denominator counts acquired paying customers consistently.
- Can the team demonstrate: spend and acquisitions are time-aligned.
- Can the team demonstrate: cOCA is paired with payback and LTV rather than interpreted alone.

Also ask:

- What observation would make the current conclusion wrong?
- Which input has the greatest leverage on the decision?
- Is the evidence from the actual beachhead and actual decision makers?
- Have incentives, selection bias, and survivorship bias been considered?
- Is the next action the cheapest action that can materially reduce uncertainty?
