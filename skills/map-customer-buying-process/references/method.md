# Map the Customer Buying Process: Method Reference

Use this reference when the core workflow requires deeper analysis. Keep the final artifact concise and move raw evidence to linked notes or data files.

## Decision objective

A validated customer-side buying process with stages, owners, artifacts, timing, dependencies, failure modes, and a realistic path to payment.

## Method notes

- Use backward reconstruction from a completed comparable purchase to reduce hypothetical answers.
- Model stage duration as ranges and identify wait time separately from active work.
- Treat any stage lacking an owner or exit condition as an unresolved research question.

## Review questions

- Can the team demonstrate: the process ends with payment and implementation readiness, not verbal interest.
- Can the team demonstrate: every stage has a customer-side owner and exit condition.
- Can the team demonstrate: budget and purchasing authority are explicit.
- Can the team demonstrate: timing is grounded in comparable purchases.

Also ask:

- What observation would make the current conclusion wrong?
- Which input has the greatest leverage on the decision?
- Is the evidence from the actual beachhead and actual decision makers?
- Have incentives, selection bias, and survivorship bias been considered?
- Is the next action the cheapest action that can materially reduce uncertainty?
